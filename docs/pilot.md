# Client pilot proposal

Buyer, price, demand and time savings remain hypotheses. No interviews or production trial have occurred.

## Five discovery questions

1. Who owns a missing requirement when preparation and client contact are handled by different staff?
2. Which approved checklist versions and exceptions must the office retain?
3. Which dates come from office policy, official rules, client commitments or external calendars?
4. What information must travel with a handoff so the recipient can act without another conversation?
5. Who can authorize stage overrides, and what evidence and retention period does the office require?

## Baseline and shadow trial

Observe one office's 15 active applications for five working days using its existing system. Measure elapsed handoff-to-first-action time, daily unassigned task count, follow-ups missed against explicitly agreed dates, and repeated entry of the same field. Record denominators and time spent maintaining the tracker.

After approving templates and access controls, run a two-week shadow trial alongside the authoritative office workflow. Reconcile outstanding items daily; require staff review of every draft and override. The tracker never submits or sends. Use synthetic or approved de-identified records until privacy and access requirements are met.

## Success and stop criteria

Proposed targets for office agreement: zero silently lost handoffs, zero silent concurrent overwrites, correct application identity in every reviewed draft/export, fewer unassigned tasks and missed follow-ups than baseline, and lower median handoff time without increasing total entry time. Report raw counts and uncertainty; do not infer broad market demand.

Stop on cross-client information leakage, unexplained missing tasks, inaccurate reminders causing consequential action, or inability to reconcile with the source system. Revert to the existing office workflow and investigate before resuming.

Next decision: if identity, ownership and reconciliation are reliable and staff see measurable value, add authenticated roles, stronger database constraints, backup/restore and approved workflow authoring. Otherwise fix the coordination workflow before adding integrations or AI.
